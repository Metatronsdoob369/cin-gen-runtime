# Intent & Boundaries Charter — Hybrid Cinematic Synthesis

**Version:** 0.1  
**Source:** `Hybrid_Cinematic_Synthesis_Charter_v0.1.docx` (imported 2026-07-19)  
**Status:** Orientation locked; architecture and implementation remain intentionally open.

**Charter purpose:** Protect the central research idea from being prematurely collapsed into a particular industry, dashboard, repository collection, model, or interface.

---

## 1. Product Intent

Create a general-purpose hybrid cinematic method that combines excellent visual references, controllable three-dimensional structure, generative video intelligence, and agent-directed filmmaking to produce coherent, high-quality video with greater control and lower dependency on chained black-box services.

The system is not defined by the subject of the footage. It may eventually serve product films, fictional worlds, architectural visualization, narrative sequences, music material, education, simulation, geospatial imagery, or uses not yet identified.

## 2. Governing Thesis

- **Structure** — Three-dimensional systems carry geometry, spatial continuity, camera authority, timing, and repeatability.
- **Appearance** — Generative systems carry atmosphere, texture, complex motion, transformation, and selective visual elevation.
- **Direction** — Agents carry planning, tool selection, evaluation, revision, and continuity across the production process.
- **Coherence** — A shared specification preserves intent and makes individual tools replaceable rather than architecturally sovereign.

**Irreducible idea:** Give generative video a controllable world, then give that world a competent AI director.

## 3. Partnership Doctrine

The human partner contributes vision, boundaries, representations, standards, and environments that make intelligent operation possible. The AI partner converts those conditions into execution, analysis, and leverage without requiring the human to become the manual memory and routing layer for the entire ecosystem.

## 4. Scope Boundary

The project is defined by the hybrid seam between deterministic world construction and generative synthesis—not by any current demonstration or downstream market.

| In scope | Out of scope at this stage |
| --- | --- |
| Primer images as a visual covenant | A real-estate or geospatial product |
| Controllable scenes, cameras, and render passes | An extension of the existing dashboard |
| Selective image-to-video or video-to-video finishing | A drone-flyby application |
| Agent planning, direction, criticism, and revision | An Unreal automation demonstration |
| Provider-neutral contracts and replaceable adapters | A wrapper around numerous paid APIs |
| Quality, continuity, cost, and reproducibility tests | A complete autonomous movie studio |
| A narrow experimental shot or micro-sequence | Custom model training before the seam is proven |

## 5. Component Status

Components are candidates with bounded roles. Compatibility or availability does not justify inclusion.

- **Unreal MCP** — Candidate execution substrate for typed scene, camera, Sequencer, render, and evidence operations; not the product identity.
- **Primer-image provider** — Candidate source of visual direction; not presumed to own continuity or final video generation.
- **Generative video model** — Candidate finishing or transformation furnace; model selection remains replaceable and empirical.
- **Cesium, Twinmotion, Blender, music, mobile, and simulation tools** — Optional adapters or downstream capabilities. None is required to define the core method.

## 6. Current Scope Freeze

Until the hybrid seam is proven, the project will not lock a target industry, interface, product name, full-length-film workflow, multi-agent hierarchy, Twinmotion dependency, mobile deployment, geospatial specialization, music-generation stack, or custom training program.

**Anti-sprawl rule:** A component enters the experiment only when it is necessary to answer the current research question or measurably improves the accepted output.

## 7. Existing Refinery Boundary

The broader ecosystem already contains a downstream-agnostic refinery that can emit vector corpora, Fine-Tune Files (FTFs), MCP services, A2A-callable capabilities, and Spectral Terrain artifacts.

**Boundary:** The cinematic project will neither recreate nor require the refinery. It may consume refinery outputs through optional, versioned adapters when pre-runtime cognitive preparation provides measurable value.

## 8. First Research Question

Can an excellent visual primer, a lightweight controllable three-dimensional scene, and a generative finishing model produce a cinematic shot that is more coherent, controllable, repeatable, and economical than either real-time rendering or image-to-video generation can produce alone?

## 9. Phase-Zero Evidence Standard

- **Comparison** — Produce deterministic-only, generative-only, and hybrid versions of the same shot intent.
- **Control** — Demonstrate repeatable camera motion, scene relationships, and bounded artistic deviation.
- **Quality** — The hybrid result must be presentable without apology and materially better than a cosmetic filter over the base render.
- **Economy** — Record compute, elapsed time, external calls, retries, and regeneration cost.
- **Traceability** — Retain the primer, scene specification, render passes, model settings, accepted output, and revision history.

## 10. Decision Test (forked-road gate)

Every proposed tool, workflow, or abstraction must answer four questions:

1. **Necessary** — Which unresolved experimental requirement does it satisfy?
2. **Visible** — What improvement should be observable in the resulting shot?
3. **Bounded** — Can it be removed or replaced without redefining the system?
4. **Proven** — What evidence will determine whether it remains?

**Orientation lock:** This project explores a general-purpose hybrid cinematic method—not any one imagined application.

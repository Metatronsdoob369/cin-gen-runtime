## 8. `stateChanging` caller-trust hazard

`stateChanging` is **caller-supplied** on `ExecuteContractRequest` (`Boolean(request.stateChanging)`).

The spine denies SAFE + `stateChanging: true`, but a **hostile or buggy admitted mutating adapter** can pass `stateChanging: false` while still mutating the world on a different path, or pass ARMED with true after forging structured approval metadata.

**Conclusion:** Unsafe as the sole mutation signal for any admitted world adapter. Future adapters need mediation that observes/controls actual mutation, not a boolean self-report.
